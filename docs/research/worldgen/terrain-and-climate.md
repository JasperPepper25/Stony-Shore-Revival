## 1. Tectonic: coherent terrain families before small-scale detail

**Verified.** Tectonic 3.0.17 expresses much of its terrain as a data-driven density graph. Its `sloped_cheese` composes a depth field, terrain offsets, separate continent/island factors, gated jaggedness, ridge weathering, dunes, roughness and ocean terrain. The density graph explicitly separates these roles instead of treating every effect as unrestricted 3D noise. Continentalness splines taper the roughness and ocean terms in complementary bands. [Sloped-cheese composition](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/sloped_cheese.json), [depth](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/depth.json).

**Verified.** Terrain region offsets use nested splines driven by erosion and a region-selector field, then a slowly varying height multiplier. The region families are named `club`, `heart`, `spade` and `diamond`, with weaker variants used in transitions. These names are internal categories, not evidence of tectonic simulation. [Region offset graph](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/terrain_spline/offset/regions.json).

**Verified.** Ridge detail is shifted using local differences between samples of a base noise and samples shifted by 10 noise-coordinate units in X/Z. The detailed-noise shifts use the absolute values of those differences, multiplied by 3000; the combined ridges are cached as an X/Z field. This is an inspectable example of making detail respond to a larger form rather than using an unrelated detail layer everywhere. It is a finite-difference construction; the source does **not** normalize it into a geometric surface normal. [Base](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/mountain_ridges/noiseshift/base.json), [shifted sample](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/mountain_ridges/noiseshift/shiftx.json), [difference](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/mountain_ridges/noiseshift/slopex.json), [shifted detail](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/mountain_ridges/shifteddetail.json), [ridge composition](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/mountain_ridges/ridges.json).

**Inference for our coast.** A shelf, arch and beach should share a larger coastal shape field. Independently randomized cutouts, sand thresholds and shelf heights can each look plausible alone while their intersection looks manufactured. Tectonic supports an architecture in which a broad form controls the amount and orientation of detail. It does not provide a ready-made ocean-facing coastal arch planner.

### Density composition and caches

**Verified.** The final Tectonic density takes the minimum of base terrain and caves before density blending, interpolation and squeezing. A noodle-cave term and underground-river/lava-tunnel terms are then combined around that pipeline. This demonstrates that the ordering of additions, cuts, interpolation and fluid-related terms is part of the implementation, not an interchangeable formatting detail. [Final-density graph](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/minecraft/worldgen/density_function/overworld/noise_router/final_density.json).

**Verified.** Repeated expressions use `cache_once`, while regional X/Z fields use `flat_cache` and sometimes `cache_2d`. The graph uses `interpolated` around the expensive terrain/cave composition. A marker is an instruction to the generation pipeline; it should not be interpreted as a general-purpose, unlimited memoization map. The source confirms marker placement, but this research did not independently benchmark every marker’s runtime effect. [Cached sloped-cheese](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/sloped_cheese.json#L1-L3), [Final density](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/minecraft/worldgen/density_function/overworld/noise_router/final_density.json), [region offset](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/terrain_spline/offset/regions.json).

**Verified optimization pattern.** `ConfigConstant` and `ConfigNoise` resolve configured values when constructed. During `mapAll`, `ConfigConstant` becomes a vanilla constant, `ConfigNoise` becomes a vanilla shifted-noise/multiply/add graph, and `ConfigClamp` becomes a vanilla clamp using the supplied bounds. This reduces the amount of custom behavior retained in the mapped graph. **Inference:** exposing our coastal field through well-formed native density nodes could help interoperability with optimizers that understand vanilla nodes, but no speedup for Stony Shore Revival has been measured. Geometry planning, biome probing and height reconstruction must still be profiled separately. [ConfigConstant](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/java/dev/worldgen/tectonic/worldgen/densityfunction/ConfigConstant.java#L20-L37), [ConfigNoise](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/java/dev/worldgen/tectonic/worldgen/densityfunction/ConfigNoise.java#L26-L49), [ConfigClamp](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/java/dev/worldgen/tectonic/worldgen/densityfunction/ConfigClamp.java#L16-L29).

**Verified newer example, absent from the reviewed 3.0.17 Java tree.** Current-head `HeightStabilizedCount` determines its number of placement attempts from vertical span divided by 16 and a `count_per_section` setting. It optionally biases attempts downward. This is useful evidence that feature density should be scaled to available habitat volume when world height changes. It is not an arch-placement algorithm, and we should not replace horizontal coastal spacing with this formula. [Current-head implementation](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/34241bdb35acda67b5367d49f354c66c05e098e2/src/common/main/java/dev/worldgen/tectonic/worldgen/placementmodifier/HeightStabilizedCount.java#L49-L60).

This is concrete integration code, not a claim that two arbitrary terrain packs can be layered without conflict. ## 3. Terralith: arches as continuous solid fields

**Verified.** Standalone Terralith’s final-density graph takes a maximum between its base terrain and `extra_terrain_sum`, followed by minimums with carving terms, then blending/interpolation/squeezing. Its arch is a density addition integrated before the final terrain surface is built, not a large prebuilt block structure placed afterward. [Final graph](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/minecraft/worldgen/density_function/overworld/noise_router/final_density.json), [extra terrain](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/density_function/overworld/extra_terrain_sum.json).

**Verified.** The arch-base field combines the absolute value of a horizontally cached shape noise, thickness adjusted by depth and 3D noise, and a cubed vertical-shape expression. A height spline driven by continentalness, erosion and temperature activates the field; `arch/total` returns a strongly negative sentinel outside the active range. This is continuous implicit geometry: noise and climate alter the field that determines where solid terrain exists. [Arch base](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/density_function/overworld/arch/base.json), [thickness](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/density_function/overworld/arch/thickness.json), [climate/height spline](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/density_function/overworld/arch/height_spline.json), [activation](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/density_function/overworld/arch/total.json).

**Limitation.** The inspected arch graph does not perform a nearby-ocean search, orient a fin normal to the coast or validate an attached cliff root. It solves a different placement problem from our reference arch. We can learn from its integration and irregular field construction without adopting its distribution or expecting it to enforce our connectivity requirements.

**Experiment.** Keep V9’s deterministic, attached, seaward fin planner, but perturb the fin’s local-coordinate solid field with bounded, spatially coherent detail. Preserve explicit portal clearance, root support, roof/web thickness and footprint limits. Compare a direct maximum at the cliff root with a narrow smooth-transition band. A smooth union is a proposal here, **not** a mechanism verified in the inspected Terralith graph. Upstream density values are not automatically signed distances; any smoothing parameter needs calibration to the two fields’ units.

### Biome “width” is a climate-mapping consequence

**Verified.** The reviewed Terralith Overworld dimension uses a noise generator and a multi-noise biome source with **1,705 parameter entries**, including **eight** `minecraft:stony_shore` entries. An early stony-shore entry occupies continentalness `[-0.2,-0.11]`, depth `[-0.005,0]`, erosion `[-1,-0.2225]`, with temperature/humidity/weirdness ranges also constraining it. Nearby entries assign some coastal climate space to basalt or granite cliffs. These entries are a mapping in climate space, not a guarantee of a fixed physical band width or a count of blocks. [Dimension mapping, first coastal entries](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/minecraft/dimension/overworld.json#L1209-L1388).

**Verified.** Tectonic separately computes biome-parameter erosion and ridges through continent/island selectors and nested splines. Therefore the same biome-parameter boxes can yield different physical layouts when the climate fields fed to them change. [Erosion parameter](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/biome_parameter/erosion.json), [paired ridges parameter](https://github.com/Apollounknowndev/tectonic/blob/b0b64b21a25ba00e5d3c7740dca6c0c7e43f7a8d/src/common/main/resources/resourcepacks/tectonic/overlay.terratonic/data/tectonic/worldgen/density_function/biome_parameter/ridges.json).

**Inference for our width question.** Enlarging a stony-shore parameter range could create additional labels but would also compete with upstream beaches, custom cliffs and inland biomes. It is not inherently the best way to recover space for a fin or shallow apron. Terrain geometry can cross a biome-label boundary while retaining the original biome assignment; biome-feature eligibility and surface treatment then need their own explicit rules. Our current bounded ocean extension is a reasonable first experiment before considering biome-map changes.

### Small wet features: another possible layer

**Verified.** Terralith’s Highlands lake uses Minecraft’s `waterlogged_vegetation_patch`, a stone ground-state provider, radius 1–2, depth 1 and extra edge-column chance. Its placed-feature chain applies rarity, square positioning, a heightmap, several nearby block predicates and a biome filter. This is a compact alternative for creating wet microfeatures after terrain shaping. It is not evidence that this patch produces our desired large elevated pools. [Configured lake](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/configured_feature/highlands/lake.json), [placement chain](https://github.com/Stardust-Labs-MC/Terralith/blob/5d7b5c58ec302c20328214f92c2a033b180dfdf2/data/terralith/worldgen/placed_feature/highlands/lake.json).

**Experiment.** Prototype a late, footprint-validated pool feature on an already generated shelf and compare it with our density/aquifer pool approach. Evaluate water retention, boundary crossing, vegetation interaction, shelf-height preservation and CPU time. Do not infer that a built-in patch’s rim checks are sufficient for a much larger basin, especially across chunks.

## 4. Larion: horizontal deformation with vertical coherence

**Verified.** Larion implements `FlatDomainWarp` as a custom density function. It evaluates the input at modified X/Z coordinates while preserving the query’s Y coordinate. It converts the warp values to integer displacements and delegates array filling directly to the context provider. Its visitor maps the input and both warp functions. [Implementation](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/java/com/badgerson/larion/density_function_types/FlatDomainWarp.java#L9-L35).

**Important distinction.** “Horizontal warp” names the **axes displaced**, not necessarily a Y-independent field. The inspected sloped-cheese warp has noise `y_scale: 0.27`; its X/Z displacement can vary at different elevations. It wraps the minimum of a river-noodle field and the Overworld depth field. Larion’s depth is a Y gradient plus a cached X/Z terrain offset. **Inference:** evaluating a height-derived field at X/Z locations that vary with Y can make the cliff profile bulge or recede at different elevations, unlike simply adding a random value to a heightmap. This is directly relevant to pronounced lips and irregular cliff profiles. [Sloped-cheese warp](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/sloped_cheese.json), [depth](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/minecraft/worldgen/density_function/overworld/depth.json), [cached offset](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/terrain/offset.json).

**Verified.** Larion builds its smaller terrain features from cliff, hill and jaggedness components. Its cliff field is generated by a combination of squared/absolute horizontal noise and erosion-dependent splines. The final density retains a blend/interpolate/squeeze pipeline with explicit top and bottom fades and cave composition. [Feature components](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/terrain/features.json), [cliff field](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/terrain/cliffs.json), [final density](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/final_density.json).

**Experiment.** Compare a bounded coastal warp with our existing additive lip geometry. Use low-frequency along-coast coherence and a smaller high-frequency perturbation; fade displacement to zero at the cliff’s attachment, pool rims and outer footprint. Keep beach elevation and shallow-water falloff monotone in the intended offshore direction. Unrestricted 3D warping can undo those constraints. Larion’s full global warp is a reference for mechanism, not recommended drop-in settings for our localized coast.

### Surface material can follow slope rather than biome color alone

**Verified.** Larion adds a somewhat-steep surface condition and changes its steep condition implementation. It checks whether the local heightmap is more than 1 block higher than a cardinal neighbor for the somewhat-steep test, and more than 3 for the steep test. Surface-rule resources use both. The cardinal samples clamp to the current chunk’s 0–15 bounds. [Somewhat-steep predicate](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/java/com/badgerson/larion/SomewhatSteepSlopePredicate.java#L13-L40), [steep predicate](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/java/com/badgerson/larion/mixin/SteepSlopePredicateMixin.java#L15-L42), [surface-rule use](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/minecraft/worldgen/noise_settings/overworld.json#L854-L902).

**Inference/limitation.** This is a useful separation of shape and material. However, a top-surface heightmap cannot see an underside, and the chunk-clamped neighbor check should not be copied as proof of seam-free slope classification. For our cliff walls and overhang undersides, local density/exposure or a terrain normal is more informative than the top heightmap alone. Sand cover should also obey our explicit beach region, rather than a general slope rule reintroducing grass islands.

### Climate layout and fluids are separate design surfaces

**Verified.** Larion’s temperature field uses a sine of a Z-coordinate term plus noise shifts, then a spline, with X/Z caching. This changes climate organization without adding new biome definitions. It is a concrete demonstration that biome layout can change by changing climate inputs. It is not a direct solution to widening one shore biome. [Temperature delta](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/temperature/delta.json), [sine](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/temperature/sine.json), [temperature spline](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/larion/worldgen/density_function/overworld/temperature.json).

**Verified.** Larion expands the reviewed noise settings to min Y −128, height 640 and alters its fluid picker to set the underground lava plane relative to minimum Y. Its router also changes floodedness and spread when river-noodle fields are active. Therefore correct shape density alone is insufficient to reproduce its river behavior: fluids are separately coordinated with that geometry. [Noise settings/router](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/resources/data/minecraft/worldgen/noise_settings/overworld.json#L18-L85), [fluid-picker mixin](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/common/src/main/java/com/badgerson/larion/mixin/NoiseChunkGeneratorMixin.java#L15-L32).

**Compatibility caveat.** Larion’s author explicitly warns against combining it with mods/datapacks that replace density functions, including Tectonic and Terralith. This is consistent with the inspected overlapping `minecraft` density/noise-setting resource paths. Treat Larion as a reference or an isolated test environment, not a newly recommended addition to our existing pack. [Author compatibility notes](https://github.com/ViciousBadger/larion-world-generation/blob/8180674462fe0706cd62854005ca57c609dd2bb6/README.md#compability-notes-with-other-worldgen-datapacksmods).

## 5. Prioritized experiments for Stony Shore Revival

These proposals preserve upstream biome placement and require measured acceptance criteria. They are research hypotheses, not committed changes to the current V9 build.

| Priority | Experiment | Why this research supports it | Measure before adopting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Record the effective density graph and integration state with every audit. | Tectonic/Terralith overlays materially remove and replace paths; filenames alone do not establish runtime behavior. | Upstream versions, active overlays, selected noise settings, interpolation configuration and biome-source identity recorded alongside seed and feature counters. |
| 1 | Share a continuous coastal shape/normal field between beach taper, fin orientation and cliff detail. | Tectonic separates broad terrain family from aligned detail; Terralith’s biome bands are climate boxes, not fixed geometric widths. | Along-coast variation, straight-run lengths, candidate rejection causes, attachment thickness and ocean-facing rate; preserve cliff mass. |
| 1 | Benchmark our original-height and biome classification probes independently from analytic feature evaluation. | Cached horizontal data is reused throughout upstream graphs, and configuration nodes can lower to native forms. | Cold/warm cache cost, cache misses per chunk, calls to upstream density, candidate planning time, memory and generated chunks/second at identical settings. |
| 2 | Compare bounded local-coordinate warping and a calibrated root-transition band for arches/lips. | Terralith forms arches inside density; Larion supplies an inspectable X/Z deformation mechanism. | Portal clearance, root connectivity, minimum roof/web thickness, unsupported voxels, seams at every chunk-boundary rotation and subjective silhouette review. |
| 2 | Compare a validated late pool feature with our density/aquifer pool. | Terralith uses small waterlogged patches; Larion coordinates fluids separately from geometry. | Wet surface area, depth distribution, actual retained water after updates/reload, erosion of rims, support integrity, elevation preservation and placement cost. |
| 3 | Only after geometry tests, investigate narrowly scoped biome replacement or climate-parameter changes. | Climate mapping controls labels and competes with other coastal biomes; it does not ensure sufficient 3D feature support. | Biome area/connected width distributions, neighboring custom biome preservation, structure/feature eligibility and unchanged terrain outside the intended region. |

Use fixed seed suites and matched mod versions for every comparison. A screenshot can establish a visible artifact but not establish which graph term caused it. Audit maps should include original versus final surface, the ocean-distance field, feature support, feature bounds, water planes and actual post-generation blocks. Interpolation and small noise amplitude must be tested together: a feature narrower than the sampled field’s effective resolution may lose its intended opening or rim.

Tectonic’s reviewed snapshot has no root license file; this review does not grant reuse permission. Implement experiments from independently designed geometry and cite the inspiration rather than copying JSON graphs wholesale.

High confidence: named source operations, fixed snapshot versions, overlay resource replacements, installed asset equivalence and inspected climate mapping. Moderate confidence: the architectural implications for our mod. Unmeasured: visual benefit, performance changes, effective current-world resource loading, physical shoreline widths and frequency of upstream landforms in a representative seed suite.
